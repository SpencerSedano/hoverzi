# Hoverzi

Hoverzi is a Chrome extension that enables seamless translation from Chinese to English by simply hovering the mouse over Chinese characters. The name “Hoverzi” is a blend of “hover”, referring to the mouse-over interaction, and “zi” (字), the Chinese word for “character.” This tool is specifically designed for learners of the Chinese language who wish to consume native web content without the constant friction of switching tabs, interrupting their reading flow, or manually copying text into external dictionaries.
